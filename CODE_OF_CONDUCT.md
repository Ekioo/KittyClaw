# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

KittyClaw is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and productive community. Be considerate, give constructive feedback, respect differing viewpoints and experiences, and focus discussion on the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption of project discussions.

## Scope

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces where someone represents the KittyClaw community.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile rather than in a public issue. Include relevant links or screenshots and enough context to assess the situation.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this code and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants when appropriate. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical. Retaliation against someone who makes a good-faith report is not tolerated.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will consider the context, severity, recurrence, and impact of an incident when deciding a response. Possible responses range from a private correction or warning to temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.
